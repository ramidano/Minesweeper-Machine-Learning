To play Minesweeper without AI:
1. Download/clone repository
2. Navigate to the the repository in your terminal and run `py game/minesweeper_pygame.py -d={1, 2, 3}`, where `-d=` represents the following difficulties: `1` = 10x10 grid with 10 mines, `2` = 18x18 grid with 40 mines, `3` = 24x24 grid with 99 mines
3. To reset/play a new game, press `R`
4. To change difficulty after launch, press `1`, `2`, or `3` for their respective difficulties
5. To close the window, press `ESC`
<br>

To play Minesweeper AI:
1. Download/clone repository
2. Navigate to the the repository in your terminal and run `py model/ai_play.py`, only difficulty `2` (18x18 grid with 40 mines) is available
3. To toggle the AI solver on or off, press `A`
4. To make the AI play only one move, press `RIGHT_ARROW`
5. To reset/play a new game, press `R`
6. To close the window, press `ESC`
